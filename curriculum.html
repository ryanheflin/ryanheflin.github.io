Practice Plan & Curriculum

Weekly training objectives, tactical principles, and development sessions.

Week 1: Build-up in the First Third

Current Focus

Focus on body shape receiving from the center-backs and finding interior passes into midfielders under pressure.

  • Tuesday: 4v4+3 Position Play Rondo & Exit Drills
  • Thursday: 8v8 Conditioned Game with High Pressing

Attacking Phase

  • Create width and depth immediately upon regaining possession.
  • Look for third-man runs in the half-spaces.

Defensive Phase

  • Immediate counter-press upon loss within 5 seconds.
  • Maintain compact vertical lines (under 30 meters).
