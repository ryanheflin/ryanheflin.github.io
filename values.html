Our Core Values

Four values guide how we evaluate ourselves on and off the field, in training and in matches.

01

Strength

Strength is physical and mental. Compete for the ball, protect your space, and play with courage. When we concede a goal, make a mistake, or face a difficult moment, we react and keep playing.

02

Discipline

Do what the team needs, even when it is difficult. Know your responsibilities, prepare well, take care of the details, and make decisions that help the team.

03

Teamwork

Football is played together. Communicate, help, cover, and share responsibility. Sometimes your best action is the one that helps a teammate.

04

Respect

Respect teammates, opponents, referees, coaches, families, the club, and the game itself. Compete at your highest level while remembering what it means to represent CE Altafulla.

Strength. Discipline. Teamwork. Respect.
They matter when things are going well, and even more when they are not.
