The referral

Simple by design.

You don't need to sell our services or determine exactly what an author needs. Make the introduction. We'll take it from there.

01

Make the introduction.

Tell us about the author, organization or book project you believe may be a fit for Wynncrest. A name and a little context are enough to begin.

02

We'll explore the project.

Our team will speak directly with the prospective client, understand the book they want to create and determine which Wynncrest services make sense.

03

If they sign, you participate.

For eligible referred engagements, Wynncrest pays a referral fee equal to 10% of the signed contract value. We handle the project. You made the connection that started it.
