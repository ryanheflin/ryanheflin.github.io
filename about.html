📜 Squad Standards & Expectations

  • 1. Punctuality: Arrive 15 minutes before practice. Late arrivals impact team tactical preparation.
  • 2. Equipment: Shin guards, water bottles, and official CE Altafulla training kits are mandatory for every session.
  • 3. Communication: Notify coaches at least 24 hours in advance if a player cannot attend training or matches.
  • 4. Respect: Zero tolerance for poor sportsmanship toward referees, opponents, or teammates.
