Welcome to the 2026/2027 Season!

Written by Coach Ryan Heflin

Welcome players and parents to the official Ex Profundo hub for our CE Altafulla U15 squad!

I built this platform to serve as our central repository for everything related to our upcoming season—from schedule updates and weekly practice plans to in-depth game statistics and film breakdown.

What to Expect Here

Rather than clogging up our WhatsApp groups with lengthy essays, I will be posting:

  • Post-Match Reports: Breakdown of tactical performance after every game.
  • Video Clips: Short 10–15 second clips analyzing key playing principles.
  • Weekly Summaries: What we worked on in training and our tactical focus for the upcoming weekend.

Stay tuned as the official FCF schedule is released. Let's make this an unforgettable season!
