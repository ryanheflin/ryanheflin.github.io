Central midfielder / Pivot · My development plan

Season 2026/27

WHAT YOU ALREADY DO WELL

You understand the game and help us play good football.

You have excellent vision, good control and the ability to find passes that connect our team. You understand where to position yourself, especially when we build from the back, and you help us play with clarity instead of rushing. These are important qualities in a central midfielder, and one of the reasons I chose you as a captain.

YOUR 2 PRIORITIES

1

BE FIRST, NOT FAST

Don't wait to see what happens before you move. Think about where the ball will go next and get into position early. When we're attacking, find the next passing angle instead of following the ball. When we're defending, recognize the danger and protect the space before the opponent attacks it.

2

BE STRONGER IN THE GAME

Don't avoid pressure or physical contact. Use your body to protect the ball, stay balanced and compete for your space. You have the size and ability to be stronger in challenges. Learn to keep possession even when a defender is close, instead of always trying to pass before contact arrives.

YOUR CAPTAIN'S CHALLENGE

Help the players around you.

You understand how we want to play. Now help your teammates understand it too. Talk to them, warn them about pressure, and help organize our midfield. You don't have to be the loudest player, but your teammates should hear you when they need you.

YOUR MATCH CHALLENGE

✓ Did I recognize where the ball or danger was going and move before it happened?

✓ Did I use my body and compete for the ball instead of avoiding contact?

✓ Did I communicate and help organize my teammates?
