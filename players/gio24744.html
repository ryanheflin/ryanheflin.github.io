WHAT I SEE IN YOU

You can become the player who helps control our game.

Gio, you understand football. You see passes, control the ball well and know how to connect your teammates. When we build from the back, you often find exactly the right position and help us play with clarity. That's one of the reasons I chose you as a captain. You show others how I want our team to play with the ball.

Now I want you to become that player throughout the entire match, not just when the game is organized. When things get fast or unpredictable, you sometimes arrive late, drift out of the action or avoid physical challenges. You have the ability and the physical tools to improve these things.

Your next step is to anticipate instead of react, compete more confidently, and use your understanding to help organize the players around you. You don't need to be the fastest player on the field. You need to see things early and make your presence felt.

YOUR MIDFIELD MINDSET

SEE EARLY → MOVE EARLY → CONTROL THE GAME

YOUR 4 DEVELOPMENT PRIORITIES

1

READ THE NEXT PLAY EARLY

Don't wait until the opponent attacks before you react. Watch where the ball might go next. When we lose possession, recognize the danger and move early to protect the center. Be in position before you need to chase.

2

FIND YOUR NEXT POSITION

During open play, don't just drift toward the ball. Think about where your teammates will need you next. After every pass, move to offer a useful angle. Stay connected to the play, even when you aren't receiving.

3

COMPETE UNDER PRESSURE

Don't always rush to pass before a defender reaches you. Get balanced, use your body to protect the ball and be willing to play through contact. When defending, get close enough to compete and make challenges rather than letting opponents run past.

4

LEAD WITH YOUR VOICE

You understand how we want to play. Now help your teammates understand it too. Call for the ball, warn teammates about pressure and help organize our midfield. Don't wait for someone else to take charge.

WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE

IN TRAINING

  • In possession games, don't follow the ball. Move early to create the next passing angle.
  • Every time your team loses possession, immediately identify and protect the most dangerous central space.
  • When receiving with a defender close, use your body and first touch to keep the ball instead of rushing to get rid of it.
  • During every exercise, communicate. Tell teammates when to turn, where the pressure is, or when they need to move.

IN MATCHES

  • When our attack moves forward, don't just follow it. Find the space where you can support the next pass.
  • When an opponent is about to receive between our midfield and defense, move to block the pass or close the space before the ball arrives.
  • When you're pressed, stay balanced and protect possession. Don't give the ball away just to avoid contact.
  • When the team loses its shape, communicate with the other pivot, defenders and nearby teammates. Help them get organized.
