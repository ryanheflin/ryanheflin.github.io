WHAT I SEE IN YOU

You can become the player who controls our game.

Gio, you understand football. You have good vision, control and passing ability, and you know how to connect our team. When we build from the back, you often find the right position and help us play with clarity. That's one of the reasons I chose you as a captain.

Now I want you to have that influence throughout the entire match. When the game becomes unpredictable, you sometimes react too late, drift toward the play or avoid physical challenges. You have the intelligence and physical ability to become much more effective. Your next step is to anticipate, compete and help the players around you stay organized. You don't need to be the fastest player if you learn to move first.

WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE

IN TRAINING

  • During possession games, move before the ball arrives to create the next passing angle.
  • When the ball changes sides, find a new position instead of following it.
  • Receive under pressure, use your body to protect possession and make the next pass.
  • On every turnover, recognize the dangerous central space and move to protect it.
  • In defensive exercises, approach early and compete rather than waiting for the opponent to act.
  • During every exercise, communicate useful information to teammates.

IN MATCHES

  • When we build from the back, continue providing good angles and passing options.
  • During open play, anticipate where the next pass will go instead of drifting toward the ball.
  • When pressed, stay balanced and protect possession rather than rushing to get rid of it.
  • When we lose the ball, protect the middle before the opponent can play through us.
  • If a teammate presses, provide cover instead of chasing the same player.
  • When the midfield loses its shape, take responsibility and organize it.
