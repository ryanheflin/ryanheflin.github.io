WHAT I SEE IN YOU

You can become the player opponents plan to stop.

Otman, I believe you have the highest attacking potential on our team. You have speed, skill, vision and the ability to create chances that other players can't. I want us to build our attack around you. Your teammates will look for you and make runs to support you.

That comes with responsibility. You need to find dangerous positions, control and protect the ball, and recognize when to attack or use a teammate. I want opponents to worry every time you receive. But I also want you to use your athletic ability to help us win the ball back. Your next step is making our whole team better, both with and without the ball.

WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE

IN TRAINING

  • When we regain possession, move immediately into a dangerous position.
  • In 1v1s, get your body between defender and ball. Don't rely only on speed.
  • Practice controlling bouncing balls with both feet, especially your right.
  • In 3v2 or 4v3 attacks, recognize when to dribble, pass or keep possession.
  • In pressing games, approach to block the inside pass and force play toward the sideline.
  • When a teammate presses, close the next passing option instead of chasing the same player.

IN MATCHES

  • When we win it, find space between defenders or behind the defensive line.
  • When receiving under pressure, secure the ball before trying to accelerate.
  • If defenders surround you, use a teammate and move again.
  • When our press begins, work with the nearest players to close passing routes.
  • When the opponent plays backward, recognize the opportunity to press together.
  • If we cannot press, recover into a position that protects the middle.
