WHAT I SEE IN YOU

You can become the player opponents plan to stop.

You have speed, skill, vision and the instinct to find dangerous space. I believe you have the highest attacking potential on our team. We're going to look for you and make runs to help you. Your job is to be available in dangerous areas, secure the ball even when you're challenged, and create chances — by beating a defender or combining with teammates. The next step isn't doing everything yourself. It's making our whole attack better whenever you're involved.

YOUR ATTACKING MINDSET

GET DANGEROUS → OWN THE BALL → CREATE AN ADVANTAGE

YOUR 4 DEVELOPMENT PRIORITIES

1

GET DANGEROUS EARLY

As we win the ball, find space to receive forward or run behind. Don't automatically drop into midfield to rescue the play. Give us a dangerous target.

2

WIN THE SPACE & FIRST TOUCH

Move your feet early, get your body between defender and ball, and control bouncing passes — especially on your right. Don't turn every duel into a footrace.

3

PICK THE RIGHT WEAPON

Once you control it, look up. If you can beat your defender, go. If a teammate has the better route, combine and move again. Keep the ball if neither is on.

4

STAY CLEAR IN CHAOS

Especially just after a regain or under pressure: see your options before you rush. One calm first touch or simple pass can set up the dangerous next action.

WHAT THIS LOOKS LIKE

IN TRAINING

  • On every turnover in a small-sided game, move to a dangerous receiving spot before calling for the ball.
  • In 1v1s, get across the defender's line while staying within playing distance of the ball; keep control rather than just knocking it past.
  • On bouncing passes, move your feet and cushion the first touch with either foot.
  • In 3v2 or 4v3 attacks, decide: beat your man, combine, or keep it.

IN MATCHES

  • When we regain it, check if you can stay high, get between defenders, or run into space behind.
  • With a defender alongside, get your body in position and secure the ball before accelerating.
  • If two defenders converge, find the free teammate, then move to receive again.
  • When the game gets frantic, don't force the first action. Control it, look, then attack the advantage.
