Otman

Season 2026/27

WHAT I SEE IN YOU

You can become the player our attack revolves around.

Otman, I believe you have the potential to become an outstanding attacking player. You have speed, skill, vision and the ability to create chances that other players can't. You recognize space, score goals and make defenders uncomfortable.

I want us to build our attack around you. Your teammates will look for you, make runs to support you and trust you to create danger. I want opponents to worry every time you get the ball.

But that comes with responsibility. You need to find dangerous positions, control and protect the ball, and make the right decision once you have it. You don't need to do everything yourself. Your job is to make our whole attack more dangerous.

YOUR ATTACKING MENTALITY

1 GET DANGEROUS
2 OWN THE BALL
3 CREATE DANGER

YOUR 2 PRIORITIES

1

ALWAYS BE DANGEROUS

Every time our team wins the ball, your first job is to get into a position where you can hurt the opponent. Find space, make runs and demand the ball.

Don't always come back toward the ball when your teammates are struggling. Stay dangerous, give them a target and trust them to find you. When defenders focus on you, use your movement to create space for your teammates.

2

OWN THE BALL. MAKE SOMETHING HAPPEN.

When the ball comes to you, take control. Don't rely only on your speed. Get your body between the defender and the ball, prepare your first touch and make it difficult for anyone to take possession from you.

Then be dangerous. Attack the defender when you have an advantage, combine with a teammate or create the next opportunity. You don't have to beat everyone yourself. Make the opponent worry about what you'll do next.

YOUR MATCH CHALLENGE

✓ When we won the ball, did I immediately look to get into a dangerous position?

✓ When I received it, did I protect the ball and create an advantage for our team?
