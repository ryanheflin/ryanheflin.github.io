Welcome to the 2026/27 Season

A first look at the environment we want to build together this season.

Hello everyone,

I want to welcome the CE Altafulla Cadete players and families to the new season.

After two years as part of this group as an assistant coach, I am looking forward to beginning this new stage together. I have had the opportunity to get to know many of the players, learn from the football and culture here, and better understand what this group needs. Now a new challenge begins for all of us.

My goal this season is to create an environment where the boys can compete, learn, and enjoy football, and where every player has the opportunity to grow both individually and as part of the team.

I do not want us to become a team that simply follows instructions. I want the players to learn how to understand the game, communicate, make decisions, and find solutions together.

There will be moments when we get things right and others when we make mistakes. Both are part of learning. What matters will be our ability to learn, adapt, and keep improving.

On my part, I will work to make sure every training session has a clear purpose and that what we work on during the week helps the players become better prepared for the problems they will face at the weekend.

The way we play will continue to evolve throughout the season as we get to know the team better and discover together what we are capable of doing.

What I Expect From the Players

I am going to ask a lot of the boys, but I do not expect perfection.

I do expect commitment, effort, respect, and a willingness to learn. I expect them to be good teammates, to communicate, to help each other, and to understand that representing CE Altafulla means something.

I also want them to learn to take increasing responsibility for their own football.

At this age, an important part of their development is beginning to think and act like footballers: arriving prepared, taking care of the small details, listening, asking questions, accepting mistakes, and responding well to difficult moments.

We want to compete. We want to win. But we also want to build something that goes beyond the result on a Saturday.

What You Can Expect From Me

If I am going to ask the players for commitment, I have to start by demanding the same from myself.

I am committed to working to give the boys a serious, positive, and demanding environment; to preparing each week with purpose; to being honest with them; to listening; to continuing to learn; and to making the decisions I believe are best for the development of the team and the players.

I cannot promise that everything will go well.

There will be difficult matches, decisions that do not work, and moments when we have to change things. That is also part of football.

What I can promise is that there will be work, reflection, and commitment from me throughout the entire season.

The Role of Families

Families have a very important role in the development of a young footballer.

My request is simple: support their growth without trying to control every part of the process.

At this age, we want the players to begin taking more and more responsibility. We want them to learn to speak with their coaches, solve problems, manage moments of frustration, accept decisions, respond to mistakes, and think about their own game.

That does not mean they have to do it alone. Quite the opposite. They need the support of their families. But sometimes the best way to help them is to give them the space they need to find their own answers.

After a match, there will be days when they have played a lot and others when they have played less. There will be excellent performances and difficult matches. There will be wins, losses, mistakes, decisions we may not agree with, and moments of frustration.

In all of those moments, I hope we can help the boys keep things in perspective.

Encourage them. Listen to them. Help them enjoy it. Ask them what they learned and how they felt. And allow them to become increasingly responsible for their own path as footballers.

Coaches and families will have different perspectives and different responsibilities, but we share something much more important: we want these boys to have a positive, demanding, and rewarding experience.

If we keep that as our starting point, it will be much easier to face everything a season brings together.

One Team, On and Off the Field

The players will be at the center of everything, but the environment we build around them will matter too.

I hope the players, families, coaches, and club can all move in the same direction.

There will be moments of joy and moments of frustration during a long season. In both cases, I want us to try to keep perspective, communicate respectfully, and always think about what helps the boys and the team continue to grow.

We have a lot of work ahead of us and still a great deal to discover about this group. That is exactly what makes the beginning of a season exciting.

I do not know exactly what kind of team we will be nine months from now.

We will build it together.

Welcome to the 2026/27 season.
